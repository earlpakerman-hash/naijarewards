About Naija Rewards

Naija Rewards is an independent rewards service for Nigerians. You complete a short task or survey, send us proof, and once we have checked it we credit airtime or data to your phone number ourselves.

We are not MTN, 9mobile, Airtel or Glo, and we are not owned by or partnered with any of them. We only ask for your network so we know where to send your reward.

We aim to keep it simple: clear steps, honest amounts, and a real person checking every claim.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

What we collect

  • The phone number you enter, your network, and the reward you chose.
  • Any proof (such as a screenshot) and message you send us on WhatsApp, including your WhatsApp number.

Why we use it

To check your proof, credit the right number, answer your questions and prevent repeat or fraudulent claims. We do not use your details for anything unrelated.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only where needed to deliver a reward, to follow the law, or to protect against fraud.

Advertising

This site shows advertisements from third parties. If you tap one, you leave our site and the advertiser’s own terms and privacy policy apply. We do not give advertisers your phone number or your claim details. Ads are labelled “Ad” and are not Naija Rewards tasks.

Task partners

Tasks and surveys may open on a third-party website. That site has its own terms and privacy policy and collects its own data. We do not control it, so read its policy before you take part.

Keeping your data

We keep claim records only as long as needed to verify and resolve claims and meet legal duties, then delete them. We aim to handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by contacting us above.

Staying safe

We will never ask for your password, bank PIN, card details or OTP. If anyone claiming to be us does, do not share it.
